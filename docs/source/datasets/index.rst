==========================
Financial Datasets
==========================

.. list-table:: Dataset Categories
   :widths: 15 45 40
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Category
     - Description
     - Datasets
   * - **Information Extraction (IE)**
     - Extracts structured insights from unstructured financial documents.
     - NER, FINER-ORD, FinRED, SC, CD, FNXL, FSRL
   * - **Textual Analysis (TA)**
     - Analyzes sentiment and interprets financial news and reports.
     - FPB, FiQA-SA, TSA, Headlines, FOMC, FinArg-ACC, FinArg-ARC, MultiFin, MA, MLESG
   * - **Question Answering (QA)**
     - Interprets complex financial queries with numerical reasoning.
     - FinQA, TATQA, ConvFinQA
   * - **Text Generation (TG)**
     - Summarizes complex financial reports and documents.
     - EDTSUM, ECTSUM
   * - **Risk Management (RM)**
     - Predicts and assesses financial risks for credit evaluation.
     - German, Australian, LendingClub, ccf, ccfraud, polish, taiwan, portoseguro, travelinsurance
   * - **Forecasting (FO)**
     - Predicts market movements based on historical data and news.
     - BigData22, ACL18, CIKM18
   * - **Decision-Making (DM)**
     - Makes informed financial decisions using multimodal inputs.
     - FinTrade
